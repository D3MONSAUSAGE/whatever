Before you start

Check your TV first, no account needed

Open pekkotv.com/pair in your TV’s web browser. If a six-digit code appears, your TV works with Pekko. Tested on LG, Samsung, Vizio and Insignia smart TVs; other screens work with a TV stick or computer.

Already have artwork?

Upload designs from Canva, Photoshop or PowerPoint, PDFs and MP4 video, and mix them with Pekko menus.
