Questions

Do I need to buy any hardware?

No, if your Smart TV has a web browser that can open secure websites. Pekko is tested on LG, Samsung, Vizio and Insignia. Otherwise, connect any streaming stick or computer with a browser to the screen.

What if the TV is turned off?

The TV stays connected to your account. Most TV browsers don’t reopen the page by themselves, so after a power cut open pekkotv.com/pair again and your menu comes back.

I already have a menu from Canva or a designer.

Upload it. Images, MP4 video, PowerPoint and PDF play full screen, and you can mix them with Pekko menus.

Can I use Pekko in Spanish?

Yes. The whole dashboard is available in English and Spanish.
