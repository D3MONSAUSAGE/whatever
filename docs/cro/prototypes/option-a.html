It worked, start my trial
Do I need to buy any hardware?

Not on smart TVs where the player has been tested, including LG, Samsung, Vizio and Insignia. Some TVs aren’t compatible; for those, a TV stick or any computer with a web browser connected to the screen works.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your screens keep playing for 3 more days. If you don’t choose a plan they pause. Nothing you made is deleted, and choosing a plan turns them back on.

I already have a menu from Canva or a printer.

Upload it. Images (JPG, PNG, WebP), MP4 video, PowerPoint and PDF all show full screen, and you can mix them with Pekko menus in a playlist.

Can I manage it in Spanish, from my phone?

Yes. Pekko’s dashboard is in English and Spanish, and it works in any phone or computer browser. Several locations and screens can be managed from one account.
