## What we collect

- Account details: email address, business name, optional display name, and a securely hashed password.

## How we use it

To run the Service: sign you in, deliver content to your screens, show screen status, send account notices you have asked for, and prevent abuse. We do not sell personal information. When a trial or plan ends, screens may pause. Your content is not deleted because of that.

- Vercel: website and API hosting.
